## What it is

A free checklist covering the eight phases of building a knowledge system: foundation and planning, then capture, organize, connect, retrieve, review, create, and finally the habits that keep it alive. Each phase is a list of yes/no items.

## Do this first

Go through the checklist and tick honestly. Count the ticks per phase. The phase with the fewest is where your path should slow down. Write that phase name in your daily note so you remember why you're doing the next module.

## Then

Redo it every three months. Same file, new date. Progress you can see is the best motivation there is.
