## Do this first

Watch the whole thing in one go. Yes, 80 minutes. It's a system, and systems don't make sense in pieces.

Then, before you close the video: open your daily note and write the time and one line about what you're about to do next. That's interstitial journaling. You've started.

## Then

- Seven days of interstitial entries. Timestamp, one line, move on. Every time you switch tasks. If you have Knowii Voice AI, speak them.
- End of the week: do your first weekly review using the questions from the workshop. Twenty minutes. Read what you wrote. You'll be surprised how much you'd already forgotten.
- Print the system diagram or keep it open. Every time the practice feels heavy, check which part you're over-doing. Usually it's the reviews. Simplify.

## Skip it if

You've been journaling daily for years, you already do periodic reviews, and interstitial journaling is old news. Then you're the exception. Watch the second half for the review structure anyway.
