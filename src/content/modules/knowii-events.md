## What it is

Everything in Knowii that happens on a calendar. Live sessions (masterclasses, workshops, Q&As, live streams), the archive of every recording, monthly community challenges, and the full archive of DeveloPassion's Newsletter. Knowledge Master members also get the exclusive sessions: AMAs, group coaching, masterminds.

## Where to find it

The events space in [knowii.net](https://www.knowii.net). The public list of past events with descriptions is at [notes.dsebastien.net](https://notes.dsebastien.net/30+Areas/34+MOCs/Knowii+Community+Events).

## Do this first

Register for the next live session, whatever the topic. Showing up live once changes how you use the community afterwards. You'll ask a question, and you'll get an answer you can't get from a recording.

## Then

- Join the current monthly challenge. It gives you a deadline for one concrete improvement to your system.
- Browse the recordings archive by topic when a module leaves you with a question. Odds are I answered it in a Q&A.
- Newsletter archive: search it. Two hundred plus editions on knowledge management, knowledge work, productivity and personal organization. It's a second knowledge base.

## Skip it if

You can't. This is the part that compounds.

## What it leads to

The next module you haven't done yet, and the one after that.
