## What it is

The first AI workshop and the foundation for the two after it. We cover how large language models work (enough to stop being surprised by them), prompt basics, then the method: a personal master prompt and a business master prompt that you paste or load into any AI so it stops giving generic answers. Ends with a live demo of my writing assistant and an introduction to MCP.

## Where to find it

Your Gumroad library, or the Knowii events archive for paid members.

## Do this first

Watch it, then write a first personal master prompt using the structure from the workshop. One page. Who you are, what you're working on, how you want answers (length, tone, format), what to never do. Paste it into Claude or ChatGPT and ask the same question you asked yesterday. Compare.

## Then

- Everything you produced in Clarity 101 goes in the master prompt. Values, principles, non-goals. That's why Clarity came first.
- Store the prompt in your vault as a note. It's a living document; you'll edit it weekly at first.
- If you use the Starter Kit: its AI assistant already implements this idea with an identity layer. The identity-layer tutorial in the user guide is the same method, automated.

## Skip it if

You already maintain system prompts or custom instructions with your full context and you understand why they work. Then go straight to the Ghostwriter guide.

## What it leads to

Build Your AI Ghostwriter, which applies the master prompt idea to one job: writing that sounds like you.
