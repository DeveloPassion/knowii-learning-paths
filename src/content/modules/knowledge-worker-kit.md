## What it is

A guide, growing since 2024, on becoming and staying a top-tier knowledge worker. Thirteen sections:

1. Clarity
2. Habits, routines, systems, processes and methods
3. Career management and anticipation
4. Learning
5. Personal Knowledge Management and journaling
6. Projects organization
7. Planning and prioritization
8. Task management
9. Focus, attention, time management and action
10. Personal development
11. Control
12. Personal organization
13. Work

It also bundles the Obsidian Starter Kit, the Starter Course, the PKM Library and every workshop recording. If you bought KWK, you own all of those. Check your library.

## Where to find it

Your Gumroad library. The guide is delivered as linked notes; the bonuses sit next to it.

## Do this first

Read the overview, then section 5 (PKM and journaling). It connects what you just learned in the course and the kit to the daily practice. Thirty minutes.

## Then

Do NOT read it front to back. Pick the section that matches the thing currently hurting:

- Can't decide what to work on: 7 (planning) then 1 (clarity)
- Too many open loops: 8 (tasks) then 12 (personal organization)
- Busy all day, nothing to show: 9 (focus and action)
- Wondering where your career is going: 3

One section every week or two. Apply one idea from each before opening the next. The guide is evergreen and gets updated; you'll come back to it for years.

## Skip it if

Nothing hurts yet. Then bookmark it and come back when it does. It will.
