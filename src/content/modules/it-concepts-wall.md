## What it is

A curated board of IT concepts and quality resources for each, organized by area: architecture, design, coding, front-end, back-end, security, infrastructure, project management. Updated when I find something worth adding.

## Where to find it

Nothing to download. After you buy, I add you to the Trello board myself, within 24 to 48 hours, using the email you paid with. Check that inbox (and spam) for the Trello invitation. Nothing after 48 hours? sebastien@developassion.be.

## Do this first

Scan the column for the area you work in. Note three concepts you can't explain in a sentence. Those are your next three things to learn.

## Then

Use it when you're about to pick up a new area. It tells you what the pieces are called so you can search for them properly.

## Skip it if

You've been around long enough to have your own map. Then compare and tell me what I'm missing.
