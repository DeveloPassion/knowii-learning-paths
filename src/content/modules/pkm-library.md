## What it is

A Notion space with everything I collected researching the PKM field: tools compared, books, courses, videos, podcasts, communities, experts, methods. Updated as I find things worth adding.

## Where to find it

**Bought on Gumroad or the store:** [your Gumroad library](https://gumroad.com/library). The Notion link is in the library. Duplicate the space into your own Notion if you want to annotate.

**Knowledge Master member:** the link comes with your welcome email. Lost it? sebastien@developassion.be. Knowii members get their invites from me personally, within 24 to 48 hours of subscribing. Nothing yet? Write to sebastien@developassion.be.

## Do this first

Nothing, honestly. Bookmark it. The library is the answer to a question, and you don't have the question yet.

## Then

Come back when you hit one of these:

- "Is there a tool that does X?" Check the tools database before installing the first thing Google suggests.
- "What should I read next about Y?" Books and articles are tagged by topic.
- "Who else thinks about this?" People and communities.

## Skip it if

You already follow the field closely. Then use it as a sanity check when you think you've found something new.
