## What it is

Two hours and twenty minutes of me walking through Obsidian and the Starter Kit: installation, interface, plugins, Markdown and YAML, then the methods behind the structure (PARA, Zettelkasten, Johnny Decimal, LIFT, atomic notes). It's the video companion to the kit's user guide.

## Where to find it

Your Gumroad library. If you have OSK Premium, the Knowledge Worker Kit, or a Knowii paid tier, it's already there.

## Do this first

Watch the Obsidian basics and the vault tour with the kit open next to the video. Pause. Click the thing I click. Passive watching is how people end up "having watched the course" without being able to create a note.

## Then

Watch the methods section (PARA, Zettelkasten, Johnny Decimal). This is the part people skip and then come back to three months later asking why their notes are a mess. Watch it now, while the vault is still small.

## Skip it if

You read faster than you watch and the user guide already clicked. The course covers the same ground with more hand-holding. Both is overkill.

## What it leads to

Knowledge Management for Beginners, which zooms out from the tool to the practice.
