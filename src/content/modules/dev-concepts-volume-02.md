## What it is

The second volume: understanding what the client or user actually needs before writing a line. Requirements discovery techniques from real projects, user-centered design, Jobs-To-Be-Done, personas and design patterns, minimum lovable products, design sprints, and specifications that prevent misunderstandings.

## Where to find it

**Bought on Gumroad or the store:** [your Gumroad library](https://gumroad.com/library). PDF in the library; updates land in the same place.

## Do this first

Read the requirements discovery chapter, then apply one technique to whatever you're building this week. Even a side project has a "client": you, three months from now.

## Then

The spec-writing chapter. Write one spec for a small feature using its structure. Show it to someone. If they understand it without asking questions, you got it.

## Skip it if

You have a product or UX background. Then use it as a shared vocabulary with the developers on your team.

## What it leads to

Back to the code, with a better idea of why you're writing it.
