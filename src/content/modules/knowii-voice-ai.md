## What it is

Voice-to-text that runs entirely on your computer. Hold a key, talk, let go, the text appears in whatever app has focus. Obsidian, your email, a chat window. No account, no subscription, no audio sent anywhere.

I put it here in the path, right after the Knowledge Worker Kit and before the journaling workshop, on purpose. The number one reason people stop journaling is friction. Speaking a daily note takes a minute. Typing it takes ten, and on day twelve you skip it.

## Do this first

1. Install, pick the model the installer recommends for your machine, set your hotkey.
2. Open today's daily note in Obsidian. Hold the key. Say what you did this morning. Release.
3. Read what appeared. Fix nothing. That's your first spoken journal entry.

## Then

- Use it for every "I should write that down" moment for a week. Capture first, sort later.
- Try it in the AI chat of your choice. Talking to an AI is a different experience than typing at it; you explain more and skip less.
- Later, once you've done the AI Master Prompt workshop, voice + a good master prompt is how I draft most of my writing.

## Skip it if

You never type more than a sentence at a time, or you can't have a microphone where you work. Otherwise, try it for a week before deciding.
