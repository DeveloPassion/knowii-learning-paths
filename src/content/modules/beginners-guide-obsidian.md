A free guide for people who just installed Obsidian, or are about to. Vaults and the `.obsidian` folder, Markdown, the interface, Quick Switcher and Command Palette, links and backlinks, the Graph view, tags, Canvas, core vs community plugins, templates, sync and backup options, keyboard shortcuts, common beginner mistakes.

## Do this first

Read the interface and links sections with Obsidian open. Create two notes and link them. Open the Graph view. You now understand the one thing that makes Obsidian different from every other note app.

## Then

Read the "common beginner mistakes" section before you install a single community plugin. Then the Starter Kit, which has the plugins already chosen for you.
