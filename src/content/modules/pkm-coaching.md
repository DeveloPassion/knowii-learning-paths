## What it is

A one-on-one session where we look at your setup, your workflow, what's stuck, and fix it together. No slides. Your screen, your vault, your questions.

## Where to find it

Book from the [store page](https://store.dsebastien.net/product/pkm-coaching). If your product came with a coaching hour (some Dev Concepts variants do), reply to your purchase email to schedule.

## Do this first

Before the call, write one note: what you tried, what happens, what you expected. Send it to me. Half the value of a session is the preparation.

## Then

After the call, write down what we decided within 24 hours. Do the first step the same day.

## Skip it if

You haven't tried the modules yet. Coaching is for the specific problem that remains after the general material, not a replacement for it.

## What it leads to

Back to your path, with the blocker removed.
